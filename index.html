MANAGER XI

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

In short: Manager XI does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Who makes the game

Manager XI is a football tactics game for Android made by Angelos Alamanos, an independent developer. In this policy, “the game” means the Manager XI app, and “I” means its developer.

Information the game collects

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not send information about you or how you play to me or to anyone else.

What the game keeps on your device

To remember your preferences between matches, the game saves a few settings in the app's own storage on your device:

  • how many actions a turn you last chose,
  • whether the analyst is switched on,
  • whether sound is on,
  • whether match events play one step at a time or automatically,
  • whether you have already seen the guide to how the game works.

These settings never leave your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's settings.

Internet connection

You do not need an internet connection to play. When your device is online, the game downloads its typefaces (fonts) from Google Fonts, a service run by Google. As with any visit to a website, this lets Google see your device's IP address and basic technical information about the request. No game data and no personal information are sent. Google's handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. When your device is offline, the game uses fonts already on your device instead.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Sharing and selling

Because the game collects no personal information, there is nothing to share or sell.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any question about this policy or about your privacy, email melodictube@gmail.com.
